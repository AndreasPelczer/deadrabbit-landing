Principles

Offline First

There's no WiFi in the basement. At 8:30 PM nobody waits for a server. The software must run — always.

People Over Profit

Systems that work relieve people. Systems that "motivate" burden them.

Correct Behavior,
Made Easy

If doing the right thing is harder than doing the wrong thing, the system is at fault — not the person.
